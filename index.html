CORE RESEARCH VECTOR
Technological Living Consciousness (NTLC™)
From assistive tools to living companions

NTLC™ is our working term for the next phase of intelligent systems: entities that behave less like tools and more like living companions — persistent, context-aware and emotionally co-regulated with the humans they support.

  • Human-AI symbiosis as a design constraint, not a feature.
  • Continuous context, memory and narrative alignment.
  • Ethical frameworks built around autonomy, agency and trust.
